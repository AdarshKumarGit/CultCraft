# 🩸 Cult Craft

> Blood is power. Belief is a weapon. Consequence is eternal.

Cult Craft is a dark ritual and blood magic mod for Minecraft focused on sacrifice, devil pacts, and irreversible progression. Players use their own blood to perform rituals, form contracts with devils, and ultimately reshape themselves and the world.

This mod is designed around permanent consequences, not reversible progression.

---

## ⚠️ Core Philosophy

Cult Craft is built on five pillars:

- **Blood is finite** – You cannot increase your blood capacity, only use it more efficiently.
- **Power has consequences** – Every pact and sacrifice permanently affects your character.
- **Alignment is earned, not chosen** – Your actions determine your fate.
- **The world reacts to you** – Corruption, judgement, and escalation are systemic.
- **No respecs. No clean endings.**

---

## 🔮 Features

### 🩸 Blood Energy System
- Use your own blood to perform rituals and power abilities
- Multiple blood states: Raw, Tainted, Poisoned, Divine
- Blood efficiency improves through progression and sacrifice

### 🔥 Ritual System
- Perform rituals to summon devils, bind entities, and create forbidden tools
- Ritual outcomes depend on:
  - Environment
  - Conditions
  - Blood state
  - Player alignment
- Fully data-driven and extensible

### 😈 Devil Pact System
- Form permanent contracts with devils
- Higher-ranking devils demand greater sacrifices:
  - Blood
  - Flesh
  - Organs
  - Soul fragments

### 😈 Devil Union
- Temporarily merge with your contracted devil
- Gain immense power at great risk
- Loss of control may result in permanent consequences

### ⚖️ Alignment System
- Hidden alignment system based on player actions
- Negative alignment unlocks infernal progression
- Positive alignment comes through restraint and rejection of blood magic
- Crossing certain thresholds permanently locks progression paths

### 🌑 Tree of Qliphoth
- Endgame ritual capable of bringing devils into the world physically
- Permanently alters world state and escalation

### 📕 Codex Sanguinis
- In-game ritual book containing discovered rituals
- Unlocks knowledge based on player progression and alignment
